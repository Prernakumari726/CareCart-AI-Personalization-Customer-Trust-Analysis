# 📊 CareCart – AI Personalization, Customer Trust & Strategy Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end business analytics case study focused on understanding how AI-driven personalization affects customer behavior and trust for a fictional D2C wellness brand called CareCart. The main objective was to study how AI adoption improved business performance while also creating risks related to privacy, transparency, and customer trust.

The project involved customer segmentation, trust sensitivity analysis, pre-AI vs post-AI impact evaluation, and the development of an AI vs Human Touchpoint Decision Matrix to identify where automation works effectively and where human intervention becomes critical.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to:

- Evaluate the impact of AI-driven personalization on customer engagement and revenue
- Identify customer segments vulnerable to over-personalization and trust fatigue
- Compare customer behavior before and after AI adoption
- Determine where AI automation should be applied and where human support is required
- Recommend a trust-preserving personalization strategy for long-term customer trust

## 📂 Datasets Used

The analysis was performed using multiple structured datasets:

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

Data exploration and cleaning were performed to ensure data quality and consistency.

Key steps included:

- Removing duplicate customer and order records
- Validating missing and inconsistent values
- Checking invalid order and age values
- Verifying data types and categorical consistency
- Creating a master dataset by integrating customer, engagement, order, and trust datasets

Additional derived fields were created for deeper analysis:

- Value Segment
- AI Response Segment
- Trust Status
- Trust Sensitivity Score
- Customer Segment
- AI Phase
- Purchase Status
- Touchpoint Type

---

## 👥 Customer Segmentation & Trust Sensitivity Analysis

Customers were segmented based on:

- Customer Value
- AI Responsiveness
- Trust Behavior

A **Trust Sensitivity Index** was developed using:

- Complaint Count
- Unsubscribe Behavior
- Privacy Queries
- Support Escalation Signals

### Key Insight

The analysis showed that a large proportion of customers belonged to **Trust Fatigue** segments despite showing strong engagement and repeat purchase behavior.

---

## 📈 Pre-AI vs Post-AI Impact Analysis

A comparative analysis was performed between Pre-AI and Post-AI periods using:

- Conversion Rate
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Complaint Volume
- Unsubscribe Rate
- Revenue Contribution

### Key Findings

- Conversion and revenue improved significantly after AI adoption
- Nearly **74% of revenue** came from the Post-AI period
- Complaint and unsubscribe rates also remained high
- Results highlighted a clear trade-off between growth and customer trust

---

## 🤖 AI vs Human Touchpoint Decision Matrix

A touchpoint decision framework was created using support escalation data to classify customer interactions into:

- Fully AI-Driven
- AI-Assisted
- Fully Human-Led

### Key Insight

- Low-empathy and high-volume queries were suitable for AI automation
- Privacy-sensitive and high-risk interactions required human intervention

---

## 🛡️ Trust-Preserving Strategy

A responsible personalization strategy was proposed focusing on:

- AI Transparency and Disclosure
- Customer Opt-in and Opt-out Controls
- Ethical Use of Customer Data
- Human Oversight for Sensitive Cases
- Continuous Trust Monitoring and Governance
